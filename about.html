The state body for the sport of cycling.

The Tamil Nadu Cycling Association governs, organises and grows competitive cycling across the state, affiliated to the Cycling Federation of India and recognised by the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu.

01Who we are

Governing and growing cycling in Tamil Nadu

The association sanctions and organises competitive cycling across the state, from district races to state championships, and selects riders to represent Tamil Nadu at national events.

  • 01
    GovernanceThe official state body for cycling, affiliated to the Cycling Federation of India and recognised by the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu.
  • 02
    CompetitionState championships, selection trials and sanctioned races in road, track, mountain bike, time trial and para cycling.
  • 03
    DevelopmentCoaching camps, officials' education and grassroots programmes that bring new riders into the sport.

02Our mission

Four commitments guide everything we do

I

Organise competition

Deliver a full calendar of safe, fair and well-officiated races across the state every season.

II

Develop riders

Identify talent early and support it with coaching, camps and a clear route to the national squad.

III

Train officials & coaches

Certify commissaires and coaches so that every event and programme meets national standards.

IV

Grow the sport

Work with schools, clubs and districts to make cycling accessible to riders of every age and ability.

04Governance

How the association is run

The association is governed by an elected executive committee and works through affiliated district associations and clubs.

  • Executive

    Office bearers

    President, Secretary and Treasurer elected by the general body, supported by vice-presidents and joint secretaries.

  • Committees

    Technical and selection

    Discipline-specific committees that oversee race regulations, officiating and selection of state teams.

  • Districts

    District associations

    Affiliated bodies in each district that run local competition and feed riders into state events.
