Sixty years on the road.

Founded in 1966, the Tamil Nadu Cycling Association governs competitive cycling across the state, affiliated to the Cycling Federation of India and working with the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu.

Riders on a closed circuit · Chennai
01Who we are

Governing and growing cycling in Tamil Nadu

The association sanctions and organises competitive cycling across the state in road, track and mountain bike disciplines, identifies talent, and selects riders to represent Tamil Nadu at national and international events.

  • 1966
    FoundedSix decades of state championships, selection trials and development work across Tamil Nadu.
  • 60
    Bicycles for schoolsOn its 60th anniversary the association gave 60 bicycles to government-school children.
  • 5×
    Rider growthRegistered athletes grew from 420 in 2021 to more than 2,050 in 2025.
  • 2026
    First UCI eventThe Track Asia Cup in Vandalur was the first UCI international cycling event held in Tamil Nadu.

02Mission 2032

Olympic gold for Tamil Nadu.

The goal is clear: Olympic medal-winning cyclists from Tamil Nadu in both men's and women's categories, and a state recognised as the cycling capital of India.

  • 01
    Elite athlete programme with SDAT18 cyclists are supported under the Champion Development Scheme (13) and the Mission Excellence Medal Scheme (5), with structured coaching, scientific support and high-performance training.
  • 02
    Residential training at Vandalur31 resident athletes, 20 boys and 11 girls, train daily at the SDAT Cycling Velodrome on the TNPESU campus.
  • 03
    A UCI-registered teamThe SDAT Racing Team is officially registered with the UCI, placing Tamil Nadu on the international calendar.
  • 04
    Coaches and officialsCFI certification pathways for coaches and technical officials so that the state can host and win at the highest level.

03Athlete pathway

First ride to national team.

A clear route for every rider in Tamil Nadu, with coaching and competition at each stage.

Stage 01 · District

Talent identification

District trials assess riders aged 12 to 18 on speed, endurance and technique.

Stage 02 · State

State camps

Intensive camps for top state riders in track, road and MTB, aligned to the national calendar.

Stage 03 · Residential

SDAT Velodrome

Selected athletes live and train at Vandalur with coaching, sports science and recovery support.

Stage 04 · International

The podium

National championships, Khelo India, Asian championships and UCI events.

04Organisation

How cycling is governed

The association operates within a national and state structure: governed by the Cycling Federation of India, supported by the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu, and run by an elected executive committee.

A National governing body

Cycling Federation of India

The apex body for competitive cycling in India. TNCA is an affiliated member and operates under its jurisdiction for road, track and mountain bike disciplines.
