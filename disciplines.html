01  ·  Road

Road

Mass-start road races, criteriums on short closed circuits and individual time trials make up the road programme. The State Road Championship is the season's flagship event and the primary selection race for Team Tamil Nadu.

  • 01
    Elite, U23, Junior and Youth categories
  • 02
    Masters categories at selected events
  • 03
    Selection for the National Road Championships
See road events
02  ·  Track

Track

Track racing takes place on the velodrome and rewards speed, tactics and teamwork. Sprint, keirin, team sprint, individual and team pursuit and bunch races are all contested at state level.

  • 01
    Sprint and endurance programmes
  • 02
    State selection trials ahead of nationals
  • 03
    Coaching camps for track-specific skills
See track events

03  ·  Mountain Bike

Mountain Bike

Cross-country mountain biking takes riders on to technical off-road trails with climbs, rock gardens and fast descents. Tamil Nadu's hill stations provide superb terrain for racing and training.

  • 01
    Cross-country Olympic (XCO) format
  • 02
    Youth and junior development races
  • 03
    Courses marked and marshalled to CFI standards
See mountain bike events
04  ·  Time Trial

Time Trial

The individual time trial sends riders off one at a time against the clock, with no drafting allowed. It is the purest test of sustained power and pacing, and a core part of the state championship programme.

  • 01
    Individual and team time trials
  • 02
    Standard and age-group distances
  • 03
    Equipment checked to current regulations
See time trial events

05  ·  Team Racing

Team Racing

Team events bring club and district squads together. From the team time trial to tactical road racing, riders learn to work for one another and build the team culture that carries into national competition.

  • 01
    Team time trial championship
  • 02
    Club and district team classifications
  • 03
    Squad training for Team Tamil Nadu
See team racing events
06  ·  Para Cycling

Para Cycling

Para cycling offers classified competition for riders with physical and visual impairments, including handcycles, tricycles, tandems and standard bicycles. The association runs state trials and supports riders through to national championships.

    Classification support and guidance
  • 02
