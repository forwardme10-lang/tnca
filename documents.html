02Rules and regulations

How we race.

All association events are governed by Cycling Federation of India and UCI regulations. The essentials for each discipline are summarised below; the full regulations apply at every event.

01  ·  Road

Road

Road races, criteriums and time trials run under CFI and UCI road regulations.

  • 01
    All riders must hold a current TNCA registration and CFI licence
  • 02
    Bicycles to UCI technical regulations; helmets mandatory
  • 03
    Junior (17–18), Under-23, Elite and Masters (30+) categories for men and women
  • 04
    Follow the marked course and traffic directions; dangerous riding means disqualification
02  ·  Track

Track

Velodrome events under UCI and CFI track rules: sprint, keirin, team sprint, time trial, omnium and madison.

  • 01
    Fixed gear, no brakes, to UCI specification
  • 02
    Skin suits and aerodynamic helmets recommended
  • 03
    Junior, Under-23, Elite and Masters categories
  • 04
    Age cut-offs follow the calendar year
03  ·  Mountain bike

Mountain bike

Cross-country (XCO), downhill and enduro formats under CFI and UCI MTB regulations.

  • 01
    Hardtail or full-suspension bikes as the discipline allows
  • 02
    Full-face helmets mandatory for downhill
  • 03
    Knee pads, elbow pads and gloves strongly recommended
  • 04
    Courses marked and marshalled to CFI standards
Full regulations: Cycling Federation of India and UCI regulations.
