02Rider registration

Who can register?

Register as an official TNCA cyclist for the 2026 season. Age is taken as on 31 December of the competition year.

1

Junior

Ages 17 to 18. The entry point to state championships and national junior selection.

2

Under-23

Ages 19 to 22. Elite racing with a dedicated development classification.

3

Elite

Ages 23 and over. Open competition and selection for Team Tamil Nadu.

4

Masters

Ages 30 and over. Age-group racing in road, track and MTB events.

Register for 2026.

Riders need a current registration to enter championship and selection events. Registration is annual and includes race insurance at sanctioned events.

  • Step 01

    Submit the form

    Send your details using the form. The office will confirm receipt and request documents.

  • Step 02

    Documents

    Age proof, identity proof and a passport photograph, plus a club or school letter where applicable.

  • Step 03

    Licence issued

    Once approved, your registration is issued and you can enter events for the season.

Questions about registration? Email cycling.tamilnadustate@gmail.com or call +91 75501 48588.
