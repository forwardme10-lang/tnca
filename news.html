Track Asia Cup brings 11 nations to Vandalur

Tamil Nadu hosted its first UCI international event. Hashini K took silver in the junior scratch race, Pranesh S bronze in the team sprint and the men's elite squad bronze in the team pursuit.

The Track Asia Cup 2026, a UCI Class-2 event held at the SDAT Cycling Velodrome in Vandalur from 29 to 31 January, drew more than 120 athletes and 30 international commissaires. It was broadcast live on News7 Tamil, Sathiyam TV and Sun News.
